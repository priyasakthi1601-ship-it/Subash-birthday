My Subash,

Happy birthday to the one person who somehow plays every role in my life at once.

You hold me up like a father would — steady, patient, always making sure I land safely even when I don't ask you to catch me. You worry over the small things like a mother would — did I eat, did I sleep, am I okay — in that quiet, relentless way that only someone who truly cares even bothers to notice. You're my closest friend, the one I tell everything to first, the one who makes the ordinary days feel like the good part of the story. And then, in the very next minute, you're a complete kid again — teasing me, making me laugh at the worst times, turning nothing into an inside joke that lasts for weeks.

I don't think I've ever told you clearly enough: I see all of it. I see how much you carry quietly and how much you give without keeping count. So today isn't just about cake and candles — it's a small thank-you for every version of you that shows up for me, every single day.

Here's to more birthdays, more inside jokes, more terrible photos we still keep, and more of us.

Yours, always —
Priya
